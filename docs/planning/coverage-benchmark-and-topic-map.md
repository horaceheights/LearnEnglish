# Coverage benchmark and topic map (A1 → A2+)

Date: 2026-11-13. Status: **analysis only**. Nothing here is an approved standard, and no lesson, spine or guardrail changes with it. Each recommendation needs its own approval before any content work.

## 1. Summary

We compared every English word in the 85 A1 lessons and the 12 A1+ Unit 1 lessons with outside word lists and syllabi.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| English word forms we teach | 400 (A1: 360, A1+ Unit 1 adds 40) |
| Oxford 3000, A1 band (898 words) | we teach 303, or **34%** (A1 alone: 279, 31%) |
| Oxford 3000, A2 band (792 words) | we teach 20, or 3% |
| Cambridge A2 Key list (1,681 headwords) | we teach 349, or 21% |
| Typical size of an A1 course | about 800–900 words (Oxford A1 band; Duolingo states about 800 per level) |

What this means:

- **A1 is thinner on words than on grammar.** A1 and the A1+ plan together cover about two thirds of the grammar the Core Inventory lists for A1 (section 2). Our vocabulary is roughly 40% of a typical A1 course.
- **The biggest holes are not topics. They are everyday adjectives and verbs.** We do not teach `big`, `small`, `good` (except in `good morning`), `bad`, `new`, `very`, `friend`, `people`, `money`, `now`, `come`, `give`, `make`, `say`, `see`, `know`, `buy`.
- **Animals are confirmed missing.** Cambridge lists about 30 animal words below A1 (Pre A1 Starters). We teach none (`chicken` and `fish` appear only as food).
- **A1+ will not close the gap at its current pace.** Unit 1 adds 32 new entries in 12 lessons. At that rate all 85 A1+ lessons add about 230 words, ending near 600. A complete A1 needs about 900.

## 3. Decisions for you

1. **`was/were`.** The Core Inventory and Ventures 1 both place the past of `be` at A1. Our 2026-11-13 decision defers it to a later course. This is a conflict with an existing guardrail, flagged here and not resolved.
2. **Contractions.** The lessons contain only `doesn't`, `let's` and `o'clock`. Every benchmark uses `I'm`, `it's`, `don't` and `what's` from the first unit. No guardrail records this as a choice.
3. **New topics and the same-number rule.** A1+ X.Y must follow A1 X.Y, so a topic with no A1 lesson (animals, school, personal details) has no A1+ home. The clean route is to add the A1 lessons first; A1 has no lesson cap.
4. **A1+ word load.** Unit 1 averages 3.6 new entries per teaching lesson against a limit of 8. Reaching a complete A1 needs about 5–6.

## 5. Grammar and vocabulary ladders

| Level | Grammar focus | Cumulative words (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| A1 (today) | `be`, present continuous, present simple with I/you/we/they, `there is/are`, demonstratives, possessives, `can`, imperatives, basic questions | 360 today; about 500 after the gap lessons |
| A1+ | third-person `-s`, `do/does`, frequency, `why/because`, possessive `'s`, object pronouns, `would like`, `-er` comparisons, regular past with `did` | about 900 (a complete A1) |
| A2 | `was/were`, irregular past, `going to`, comparatives and superlatives, `have to`, `should`, `could`, `will`, countable and uncountable | about 1,300 |
| A2+ | past continuous, present perfect with `ever/never`, `will` vs `going to`, first conditional, common phrasal verbs, verb + `-ing` or infinitive | about 1,700 (a complete A2) |

The A2 and A2+ grammar rows follow the Core Inventory A2 list. The word targets follow the Oxford bands (A1 898, A1+A2 1,690) and the Cambridge A2 Key list (1,681).

## 7. Sources and limits

Read and used:

- Oxford 3000 by CEFR level (Oxford University Press), from a public copy of the list.
- Cambridge A2 Key and Key for Schools Vocabulary List, 2025 update.
- Cambridge Pre A1 Starters, A1 Movers and A2 Flyers word list, 2018 (thematic list).
- British Council / EAQUALS Core Inventory for General English, 2011.
- Cambridge Ventures 3rd edition, Basic and Level 1 scope and sequence.
- CASAS competency list, May 2008 version.
- Duolingo, "How are Duolingo courses evolving" (about 800 words per CEFR level).

Not obtained, so not reflected here: Interchange Intro (publisher file timed out), English File Beginner (page blocked), Headway, Side by Side, Babbel, Busuu, Pearson GSE, the Cambridge English Vocabulary and Grammar Profiles, and the New General Service List. Recent-trend research was limited to the Duolingo article.

Limits of the numbers:

- Word matching is automatic. It joins simple inflections (`book/books`, `eat/eating`), so each percentage can be off by a point or two.
- A word counts as taught if it appears anywhere in a lesson, including once in a review.
- A1+ is measured on Unit 1 only, the only unit built.
- The outside lists are British-leaning (`trousers`, `colour`). American equivalents we teach (`pants`, `color`) show as missing for those few words.
